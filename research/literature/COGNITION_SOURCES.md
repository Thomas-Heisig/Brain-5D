# Kognitionsprogramm: Quellen und tatsaechliche Nutzung

Stichtag: 7. September 2026. Gezielte Ergaenzungsrecherche, keine vollstaendige systematische Datenbankreview. Die Auswahl folgt den Kritikthemen. Treffer-/Screeningzahlen werden nicht erfunden. Suchwege: Websuche, direkte Verlags-/Institutionen-HTML-Aufrufe, PubMed-Abstracts und offizielle Aufgabendokumentation; Repositorylekture separat. Keine native Vollsuche in Scopus, Web of Science oder PhilPapers.

Zugang, gelesener Umfang und argumentative Verwendung stehen pro Quelle. Metadaten werden nicht als Volltextlekture ausgegeben. Keine der Quellen validiert Brain-5D; kein klinischer Datensatz wurde hier neu ausgewertet. Amtliche Rechtsabrufe hatten bei Wiederholung teilweise Timeouts; dies ist ausgewiesen. Die Bibliografie ist ein konservativer Austausch-Nachtrag, kein vollstaendiger bibliometrischer Audit.

<a id="src-cns-butlin"></a>
## SRC-CNS-BUTLIN

Patrick Butlin and others (2026). *Identifying indicators of consciousness in AI systems*.

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1364661325002864

**Gelesener Umfang:** Publisher metadata and relevant abstract/search-accessible passages; direct full-page retry blocked.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Theory-dependent indicators; no universally sufficient consciousness test. Online 2025, journal issue June 2026; not a Brain-5D validation.

<a id="src-cns-cogitate"></a>
## SRC-CNS-COGITATE

Cogitate Consortium and others (2025). *Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness*.

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1

**Gelesener Umfang:** HTML article, relevant theory/comparison passages.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Contrasting predictions and adversarial testing; no consensus that one architecture is necessary for all possible artificial consciousness.

https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niaf037

<a id="src-cns-seth"></a>
## SRC-CNS-SETH

Anil K. Seth (2025). *Conscious artificial intelligence and biological naturalism*.

https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X25000032

**Gelesener Umfang:** Accepted-manuscript abstract/metadata.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Biological-naturalist counterposition; its substrate assumptions are a theory position, not an established impossibility theorem.

<a id="src-cns-welfare"></a>
## SRC-CNS-WELFARE

Robert Long and others (2024). *Taking AI Welfare Seriously*.

https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00986v1

**Gelesener Umfang:** HTML preprint, relevant precaution and uncertainty sections.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Precaution under uncertainty; preprint, no proof of current AI sentience.

<a id="src-cns-oddball"></a>
## SRC-CNS-ODDBALL

Tristan A. Bekinschtein and others (2009). *Neural signature of the conscious processing of auditory regularities*.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19164526/

**Gelesener Umfang:** PubMed abstract and metadata.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Local/global auditory regularities and attention/report conditions; no diagnostic transfer to AI.

<a id="src-cns-pci"></a>
## SRC-CNS-PCI

Adenauer G. Casali and others (2013). *A theoretically based index of consciousness independent of sensory processing and behavior*.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23946194/

**Gelesener Umfang:** PubMed abstract and metadata.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Clinical perturbational TMS-EEG method. No clinical cutoff transferred to SNN proxies.

<a id="src-cns-meta"></a>
## SRC-CNS-META

Brian Maniscalco and Hakwan Lau (2012). *A signal detection theoretic approach for estimating metacognitive sensitivity from confidence ratings*.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22071269/

**Gelesener Umfang:** PubMed abstract and metadata.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Meta-d-prime is distinct from type-2 AUROC or Brier score; validated fitter remains a separate implementation requirement.

<a id="src-cns-lfp"></a>
## SRC-CNS-LFP

Alberto Mazzoni and others (2015). *Computing the Local Field Potential (LFP) from Integrate-and-Fire Network Models*.

https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004584

**Gelesener Umfang:** HTML full text, relevant observation-model passages.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** LFP requires a justified observation model; scalp EEG requires an additional forward model and montage specification.

<a id="src-cns-mask"></a>
## SRC-CNS-MASK

Michael A. Cohen and others (2024). *Neural signatures of visual awareness independent of postperceptual processing*.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39535504/

**Gelesener Umfang:** PubMed abstract and metadata.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Report/no-report and masking dissociations; not a universal neural consciousness signature.

<a id="src-cns-neurogym"></a>
## SRC-CNS-NEUROGYM

NeuroGym contributors (2026). *NeuroGym environment API documentation*.

https://neurogym.github.io/neurogym/latest/api/envs/

**Gelesener Umfang:** Official environment documentation and task listing.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Reproducible task-implementation reference, not a certified consciousness battery. Future adapter must pin a concrete package commit/version.

<a id="src-cns-turing"></a>
## SRC-CNS-TURING

Alan M. Turing (1950). *Computing Machinery and Intelligence*.

https://academic.oup.com/mind/article/LIX/236/433/986238

**Gelesener Umfang:** Publisher HTML/metadata and imitation-game framing.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Behavioral imitation game, not a test proving phenomenal experience.

<a id="src-cns-arc"></a>
## SRC-CNS-ARC

Francois Chollet (2019). *On the Measure of Intelligence*.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01547

**Gelesener Umfang:** Preprint abstract and metadata.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Skill-acquisition/generalization framework and ARC; this supplement does not claim current ARC benchmark performance.

<a id="src-cns-lsnn"></a>
## SRC-CNS-LSNN

Guillaume Bellec and others (2018). *Long short-term memory and learning-to-learn in networks of spiking neurons*.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09574

**Gelesener Umfang:** Preprint abstract and metadata.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Prior spiking-network work with adaptation and longer memory timescales; slow/fast loops alone are not new.

<a id="src-cns-neurons"></a>
## SRC-CNS-NEURONS

Frederico A. C. Azevedo and others (2009). *Equal numbers of neuronal and nonneuronal cells make the human brain an isometrically scaled-up primate brain*.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19226510/

**Gelesener Umfang:** PubMed abstract and metadata.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Human neuron-number estimate from a biological sample, not a minimum-size criterion for artificial experience.

<a id="src-cns-equiv"></a>
## SRC-CNS-EQUIV

Daniel Lakens (2017). *Equivalence Tests: A Practical Primer for t Tests, Correlations, and Meta-Analyses*.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28736600/

**Gelesener Umfang:** PubMed abstract and method summary.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Prespecified equivalence bounds; nonsignificant difference is not demonstrated equivalence.

<a id="src-cns-blink"></a>
## SRC-CNS-BLINK

Jane E. Raymond and Kimron L. Shapiro and Karen M. Arnell (1992). *Temporary suppression of visual processing in an RSVP task: an attentional blink?*.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1500880/

**Gelesener Umfang:** PubMed abstract and metadata.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** Dual-target temporal attention paradigm; an artificial blink can be an engineered bottleneck rather than evidence of experience.

<a id="src-cns-stop"></a>
## SRC-CNS-STOP

Frederick Verbruggen and others (2019). *A consensus guide to capturing the ability to inhibit actions and impulsive behaviors in the stop-signal task*.

https://elifesciences.org/articles/46323

**Gelesener Umfang:** HTML recommendations, race-model assumptions and analysis passages.

**Verwendung und Grenze:** SSRT validity depends on task/estimator assumptions; no substitute for a hardware emergency-stop test.
